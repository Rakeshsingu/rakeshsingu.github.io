Privacy Policy

GymLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Positioning: "Your data stays on your phone."

1. Core Principle: Local-First

GymLog does not collect, store, or sell your personal data. There are no user accounts, no developer servers, and no background tracking. All workouts, bodyweights, nutrition logs, and personal records remain exclusively on your device.

2. What Data Is Stored and Where

  • Workout Logs & Weights: Stored locally in an on-device database.
  • Body Measurements & Targets: Stored locally on your device.
  • Coach Notes: Stored locally on your device.
  • Backups: You can export an offline JSON file to your device or share it. Daily automatic backups are saved locally on your phone (in your Downloads folder).

3. Cloud Backups (Google One & iCloud)

GymLog enables standard operating system backups (Google One on Android and iCloud on iOS) so you don't lose data if you switch or restore your device. These backups are stored in your personal Google/Apple account. We have no access to your cloud backups or account credentials.

4. Third-Party AI Services

GymLog is a "bring-your-own-AI" app. GymLog prepares prompt summaries on your device. Nothing is sent over the network automatically. Data leaves your device only when you explicitly copy or share a prompt into an external AI app (such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini) of your own choice. User names are never included in prompts.

5. Purchases & Billing

GymLog Pro is a one-time purchase processed directly through Google Play In-App Billing or Apple App Store In-App Purchases. We do not process, store, or receive your financial details or payment information.

6. Analytics, Ads, and Tracking

GymLog contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero analytics trackers, and zero crash telemetries. App Store and Google Play privacy labels reflect: Data Not Collected.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via email at: support@samasra.net.
